    UNDER THE SURFACE

    Uncertainty,
    made visible.

    The engine runs offline and publishes one versioned snapshot. Every visitor reads the same precomputed results; changing the horizon never starts a new simulation.

    What the numbers mean

    Touch means the price reaches your target at least once. Finish means it ends beyond your target. Target before stop counts paths where the target is reached first. The median touch time only includes paths that hit.

    What the chart means

    The shaded area spans the 10th to 90th price percentiles at each time. It is not a promise that 80% of entire paths stay inside the band. The uncertainty range tests alternative model assumptions; it is not a validated confidence interval.

    What is—and is not—validated

    The supplied demo uses synthetic data. Calibration requires held-out historical observations and a live track record. No accuracy claim is made. News, geopolitics and fundamental data should remain context-only until their effects pass out-of-sample tests.

    Price and time

    Simulation begins at the snapshot reference price, not your entry. Targets and stops are limited to the published grid. Time windows are calendar horizons with trading time inside them; exchange holidays and extraordinary closures require an updated calendar. Free data can be delayed or stale.
